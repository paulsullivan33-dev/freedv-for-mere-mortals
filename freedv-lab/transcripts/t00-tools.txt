$ cmake ../src/codec2-1.2.0 -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release -DLPCNET=OFF -DUNITTEST=OFF && make freedv_tx freedv_rx c2enc c2dec
(official codec2 1.2.0 source, same version as the distro package; LPCNet off, so the 2020 modes are not built)
